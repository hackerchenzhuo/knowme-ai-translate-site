知我AI翻译 · 2.4

记住你的专业译法，让下一段更合你的习惯。需要 Chrome 116 或更新版本，使用你自己的模型 Key 或获准使用的公司服务。

三步快速入门

首次安装可选“开始真实体验”，也可“直接使用”跳过。以后在设置底部点“快速入门”重开。

  1. 翻译示例：使用你配置的模型，消耗正常额度。
  2. 纠正一个词：确认原词、偏好译法和领域，点击“仅记住”。
  3. 翻译下一句：实际沿用时，点带下划线的词查看来源；再用不同语境检查适用范围。

“这段合适 / 需要调整”是在评价译文，选好再点“确认反馈”；“纠正译法”才是明确保存某个词怎么翻。评价不会自动重译。

从连接开始

点击工具栏的知我图标，再点齿轮进入设置。选择厂商，填写模型 ID、服务地址和对应 Key。支持 OpenAI、Google、Anthropic、Moonshot、Zhipu、ByteDance、DeepSeek、Alibaba Cloud、xAI 和公司兼容网关；模型预设可编辑，不代表你的账号一定拥有访问权限。公司网关须支持 OpenAI Chat Completions。不要把 Jev 填进翻译模型框。

点击“保存并测试连接”可保存配置，并发送一次固定的 Hello 翻译请求。最多等待 20 秒，可停止；不发送原文历史、个人术语或自定义提示，不增加翻译记忆或学习记录。成功只说明当前请求得到完整文本回复，不认证翻译质量。模型服务可能收费。普通“保存设置”和回车保存不会自动测试。

切换厂商或服务地址需使用相应 Key，不带出旧服务的 Key。连接与记忆在当前浏览器加密保存，无开发者账号。首次设置本机口令；浏览器重启或扩展更新后解锁一次。请记住这个口令，忘记后无法恢复加密记忆。升级时沿用原安装目录，重新加载扩展后刷新已打开的阅读网页。

划词、快捷键与窗口

  • 网页选中文字，点击小“译”按钮；设置可改为直接翻译、双击或按住修饰键触发。
  • Cmd+D（Mac）/ Ctrl+D（Windows）在网页有选区时翻译选区；无选区时不拦截，仍是浏览器的“加入书签”。如需无选区也打开独立窗口（已经允许剪贴板读取时读取一次并翻译，未授权则在窗口点击“粘贴并翻译”），可在设置中把页面快捷键改为 Alt+D 或 Cmd+Shift+Y。没有后台剪贴板监听。
  • 在阅读窗口里，快捷键优先使用当前输入。改动原文后点击“翻译”或按 Cmd+Enter / Ctrl+Enter；输入过程不发请求。
  • 普通文本框和可编辑正文中的明确选区可翻译；密码框及专业代码编辑器不截获网页快捷键。
  • 浮窗右上角 ↗ 把已有译文带到独立窗口，不重复请求；右键菜单也可翻译选区。
  • 浏览器级快捷键可在设置中查看、跳转配置。默认 Cmd+Shift+Y（Mac）/ Alt+Shift+Y（Windows）；若改成 Cmd+D，请绑定“打开知我翻译窗口”，而非“激活该扩展程序”。其他扩展的冲突绑定需要在 Chrome 中移除。
  • 可设置连按三次 Cmd / Ctrl，以及将新选区送入已打开的独立窗口。

网页浮窗可拖标题栏、固定、拖四边或四角缩放；位置与尺寸在松手后保存。Esc 先收起打开的操作菜单，再关闭未固定的浮窗。独立窗口使用浏览器原生拖动与缩放。工具栏弹窗由 Chrome 定位，需要自由移动时点 ↗。

网页快捷键需要启用划词、网站权限和刷新后的页面。Chrome 内部页、内置 PDF 阅读器等受限页面不能注入浮窗，可复制到独立窗口。系统桌面和全屏策略可能影响独立窗口出现的位置。

语言、风格与排版

源语言和目标语言可手动选择。自动模式在首选语言与备用语言之间转换；判断不准时直接指定。交换语言会把完成的译文放回输入区，点击翻译再提交。

“原文语气 / 商务表达 / 清晰易读 / 学术严谨”是表达风格，不是专业领域。更换语言或风格后，非空原文会按新选择翻译；精确缓存存在时直接复用。原意、否定、不确定性、数字和引用始终应保留。

Markdown 标题、列表、引用、删除线、代码及表格直接呈现。结构明确的重复记录可整理为表格，字段不齐的记录使用紧凑卡片；只标注原文确实缺少的字段，不补造缺失信息。无法可靠识别时保留原顺序。流式完成后再整理结构，减少表格跳动。

表头使用浅灰底，宽表在表格内滚动，深色模式同步适配。数字未找到完整对应时出现可展开的“数字核对”；它可能提示合法的数字写法变化，不认证整体语义质量，也不自动改文或重试。

支持 TXT / Markdown 文件（最多 200 KB），每次最多 20,000 字符。超长粘贴和文件内容完整保留，并提示实际长度，需要主动缩短后才发送。超长网页选区会提示缩小选区，不转发截断片段，也不覆盖已有草稿。暂不支持 OCR、整份 PDF 上传或系统级取词。

复制与重新翻译

“复制译文”复制完整的可读内容；悬停段落、列表、表格或代码块，或用 Tab 聚焦，可复制单块。表格按行和制表符分列，代码保留缩进。⋯ 菜单中的“复制 Markdown”保留原始 Markdown 标记，不包含界面按钮与记忆标注。

⋯ →“重新翻译”只刷新本次请求，不清除其他缓存。新文字到达前保留旧译文；请求失败或在首字出现前停止，仍能继续阅读旧结果。限流不会自动重试。

复制不请求模型，也不代表你认可译法，不会自动新增个人偏好。

译文缓存

新安装默认保留 30 天，可设 1–365 天；升级保留原设置。最多 500 条 / 约 6 MiB，达到上限淘汰较旧记录。按生成时间过期，查看或缓存命中不续期。失败、取消和不完整的结果不写入成功缓存。

缓存区分原文、语言、实际提示、模型、服务和账号；相同请求直接复用，同时到达的相同请求合并。相关偏好变化会生成新结果，无关术语不使本段缓存失效。缓存不是一次新的学习，也不是服务提供方的 prompt/chat cache。

“翻译记忆”可搜索、阅读旧结果，不请求模型。关闭“存入翻译记忆”停止自动复用和新增，已有记录保留。单条“移除”在 60 秒内可撤回上一次；不延长缓存有效期，不覆盖更新结果。清空译文不删除个人术语或反馈。历史结果保留生成时的语言、风格和记忆来源，修正重译沿用该条上下文。

按领域记住你的译法

点击译文下“纠正译法”，填写原文术语、偏好译法和领域。选中原文或译文中的短语可快速填入。“记住并重译”保存后刷新本段；“仅记住”保存供后续内容使用，不再次调用模型。

系统在本机根据原文线索建议领域。领域不明确或冲突时不套用专门译法；“通用 · 跨领域”允许跨领域使用，“仅相似语境”要求至少两条保存的上下文线索。支持 AI、医学、金融、法律、房地产、自然科学，以及自己的课题领域。这是保守的局部匹配，不是通用语义理解。

实际出现保存的目标词时，显示“已沿用你的译法”和词语对应；正文首次出现处加淡下划线。点击可查看保存时间、领域、手动或 AI 建议来源，再修正或撤回。模型未使用目标词时不宣称已沿用。缓存沿用原始来源，不作为新学习。

表达与排版习惯在设置中按领域保存。个人偏好不随缓存过期，最多 12 个自定义领域、100 个术语版本、40 个表达习惯版本及 200 条变更记录。每项最多保留 12 个上下文词；普通请求附加偏好总计最多 512 UTF-8 字节，不发送完整历史。

备份与迁移

设置 → 个人译法 → 备份与迁移，可导出术语、领域关键词和表达偏好。JSON 是明文，不含 Key、原文历史、反馈、学习证据或使用次数；文件中的专业词语仍属于你的数据。

导入前先显示新增、重复与冲突项，确认后才保存到当前已配置的连接；冲突保留已有译法，不覆盖。导入项作为手动偏好，原先试用或暂停的条目保持暂停，不恢复旧的晋级证据。10 分钟内可撤回本次新增。文件最多 128 KB，非法或超量文件不会部分导入。备份不等于云同步。

反馈与有限 AI 建议

先选“这段合适”或“需要调整”，再点“确认反馈”保存；可取消草稿、修改或撤回。整段反馈不会立即变成术语偏好。普通阅读、复制与无操作不视为认可。

在同一目标语言、风格和领域积累三段不同反馈，其中至少两段合适或明确纠正后，可在设置点击“生成建议 · 1 次 AI 请求”。模型仅能提出短术语或预设表达/排版选项，须引用至少两段有效依据。没有充分依据时允许不提出建议。检查后“确认并记住”才供后续相关内容使用；随时可撤回。

自动建议默认关闭，开启后只在翻译或反馈后的空闲机会整理，没有持续后台任务。主动与自动共用每个连接每 24 小时一次：最多六段、8 KiB 输入、请求输出上限 1,024 token、20 秒，无自动重试。失败、停止、空建议也可能计费并占用额度。新翻译优先停止建议请求。

最多 60 段反馈，保留 90 天。删除反馈也删除依赖的建议和证据副本；已确认的译法可单独撤回。切换服务或账号会关闭自动建议。它不能修改模型、Key、基础提示、代码或权限，不训练模型权重，也不承诺翻译质量自动提高。

术语试用核对（高级）

手动记忆是默认方式。需要试用核对时，先保存连接与翻译设置，点“检查当前模型 · 2 次请求”；通过后选择有限学习并保存。检查只验证两条合成文本中的术语、数字、代码，不认证完整语义质量，不要求特定模型规模或工具能力。

每次最多附加 2 条术语、其中最多 1 条试用，共享 512 字节偏好上限。最多 3 条并行试用，每条最多 5 个不同后续原文；3 次通过保留，连续 2 次失败或预算耗尽则暂停。缓存、重复段落和原始纠正样本不计证据。这些核对在本机完成，不增加翻译调用。

模型检查每条最多 4,096 输入字节、请求 1,024 输出 token、15 秒超时；每 24 小时最多三轮，至少间隔一分钟。检查有效 30 天，相关配置变化后失效。通过不自动启用学习；手动模式不应用试用项。

关联阅读与可选 Jev

共同术语、代码、文献标识，或明确领域内的共同短语，可以形成历史关联。只有有内容时显示入口；展开后是“当前译文 → 关联依据 → 历史片段”，最多三条。单个模糊词不构成联系。点击阅读不请求模型，“不相关”可隐藏这对联系。它不是因果关系，也不会自动把历史译文加入当前提示。

Jev 核对默认关闭，需独立 GLINK Key 与 generic 地址（默认 matrixcube.alipay.com/v1/genericCall）。固定模型 jev-1-13、方法 /decisions，不复用翻译 Key。主动点击才核对一条待确认建议，或另行启用译文后关联核对、一次最多三项。总输入最多 8 KiB、3 秒、每 24 小时共三次，失败或停止也计数，无自动重试；相同关联结论复用 24 小时。

Jev 只返回支持、不匹配或依据不足，不改变译文、偏好或关联，支持也需你确认。现有评测不足以证明普遍提高翻译质量，因此保持实验性可选功能，不加入普通翻译的等待流程。

常见问题

快捷键无响应：查看实际浏览器快捷键及其他扩展冲突，检查启用划词、网站权限和页面刷新。界面与后台版本不一致时按提示重新加载。

HTTP 429：按服务返回信息区分频率限制与额度不足；有 Retry-After 时显示等待。仅凭状态码不能判断具体原因。等待结束只恢复手动操作，不自动发送。

翻译慢：界面显示服务已经返回的文字，网络等待与模型生成需分别判断。普通翻译最多 90 秒，可主动停止。不要把一次网络测试视为网关整体速度结论。

界面默认中文，可在设置改为 English 或跟随浏览器；不改变原文、模型输出或个人术语。KnowMe 是独立项目，非 Saladict 或 Google 官方产品。应用代码独立实现，依赖许可随扩展提供。

KnowMe AI Translate · 2.4

A personal translation reader for Chrome 116+, using your own model key or an authorized company gateway.

Optional quick start

First install offers a skippable guide: translate, explicitly save one term, then inspect its actual use in a new passage. Reopen it from Settings. This uses your configured model and provider quota. A rating saves your evaluation only; a correction saves specific wording for later use.

Connect and read

Open the toolbar icon, then Settings. Choose OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Moonshot, Zhipu, ByteDance, DeepSeek, Alibaba Cloud, xAI or an OpenAI-compatible company gateway. Presets are editable; availability depends on your account. A new provider or endpoint requires its own key.

Set a local passphrase to encrypt saved keys and translation memory. It is separate from your Google password and is never sent to a service. Unlock once after restarting the browser or updating the extension. A webpage panel opens an extension window for unlock; it never asks for the passphrase inside the webpage. Forgotten passphrases cannot recover encrypted memory. Existing local installations should keep the same extension directory and ID when reloading.

Save & test connection saves your settings and sends one fixed Hello translation, with a 20-second timeout and a Stop button. It sends no history, personal terms or custom instructions and creates no memory or learning record. A pass means a complete text response, not translation-quality certification. Ordinary Save and Enter do not run a test. Provider charges may apply.

Select text and use the small translate button, configured page shortcut or context menu. Cmd+D / Ctrl+D translates a page selection; with no selection it is left to the browser's bookmark command. With Alt+D or Cmd+Shift+Y as the page shortcut, no selection opens the reader and reads the clipboard once only if permission already exists. Use Paste & translate for the first permission request. Inside the reader, the current draft has priority. There is no background clipboard monitoring.

Edit the visible source and press Translate or Cmd+Enter / Ctrl+Enter; typing alone sends nothing. Native browser shortcuts are shown in Settings. Assign Open reader, not Activate extension, and resolve competing extension shortcuts in Chrome. Refresh reading pages after an extension reload. Password fields and code editors retain their page shortcuts.

Drag the webpage panel by its title bar and resize any edge or corner. Position and size save on release. Escape closes a menu before the unpinned panel. The separate reader uses native window controls. Open in window transfers a completed result without generating it again. Browser-internal and built-in PDF pages cannot host the panel; paste their text into the reader.

Language, style and format

Choose languages explicitly when automatic detection is wrong. Original tone, Business wording, Clear wording and Academic precision are style presets, not domains. Changing language or style translates nonempty input using that selection; an exact cache hit is reused.

Markdown headings, lists, tables, strikethrough and code render directly. Clear repeated records may become tables; incomplete records use compact cards. Missing information is never invented by the local formatter. Structure settles after streaming. Wide tables scroll inside their own frame. Local number checks flag unmatched values without silently changing or retrying text; legitimate formatting changes can also trigger a flag.

Open TXT/Markdown files up to 200 KB; each request allows 20,000 source characters. Oversize pasted text and files are retained in full and blocked from sending until shortened. An oversize webpage selection prompts you to shorten the selection; no truncated passage is forwarded and existing drafts are preserved. Whole-PDF upload, OCR and system-wide text capture are not included.

Copy the entire result or hover/focus a paragraph, list, table or code block to copy that block. More → Copy Markdown preserves raw Markdown. Copying is not approval and creates no preference. More → Translate again refreshes this request only; other cached passages remain. The previous result stays readable until new text arrives, including failure or cancellation before the first new chunk.

Cache, personal wording and feedback

Fresh installs retain exact successful results for 30 days, adjustable to 1–365; upgrades retain their setting. Limits are 500 entries / about 6 MiB. Age starts at generation and is not renewed by reuse. Source, languages, actual instructions, model, endpoint and account separate cache identities; concurrent identical requests share generation. Failed or incomplete output is not cached. Unrelated new terms do not invalidate a passage.

Translation memory supports search and reading without a model call. Remove one record with a 60-second undo of the last removal; restoring never extends expiry or overwrites a newer result. Disabling Remember translation stops automatic reuse and new saves. Clearing translations does not delete personal wording or feedback. Cache is not learning or provider prompt caching.

Correct wording saves a source term, preferred translation and domain. Remember & translate again refreshes this passage; Remember only affects future matching passages. Source or output selection can prefill the editor. Conservative local cues select relevant domains; ambiguous evidence skips specialized wording. Similar contexts needs two saved context cues, while General is explicitly cross-domain.

When the preferred text actually appears, a subtle underline and term pair show its origin. Click to inspect or revise it. Missing wording is not called adopted. Cached output preserves its original receipt. Writing/layout choices live in Settings. Up to 12 custom domains, 100 term versions, 40 style versions and 200 change events are retained. Personalization adds at most 512 UTF-8 bytes per request, not a full history.

Backup & migration exports plain JSON containing terms, domain cues and style choices, without keys, source history, feedback, learning evidence or usage counts. Preview before confirming import; existing conflicts are kept and duplicates skipped. Imported entries are manual preferences, with prior trials/paused entries remaining paused. Undo newly imported entries within ten minutes. Maximum file size is 128 KB; invalid files are not partially imported. This is not cloud sync.

Choose Works for me or Needs revision, then Confirm feedback. Cancel, edit or undo anytime. Passage approval is not an immediate glossary rule, and copying never implies approval.

After three distinct feedback passages in the same language, style and domain, including two positive or corrected examples, Find suggestions makes one model request. It may propose short term pairs or preset style choices with at least two supporting excerpts. Review and confirm before later translations use it. An empty result is valid.

Automatic suggestions are off by default. Manual and automatic runs share one attempt per connection per 24 hours: at most six excerpts, 8 KiB input, 1,024 requested output tokens and 20 seconds, without retries. Failed, stopped or empty runs count and may be billed. New translations cancel suggestion work. Feedback is limited to 60 passages / 90 days. Deleting it removes dependent proposals and evidence; confirmed terms remain separately manageable. Changing account or service disables automatic suggestions.

This adapts bounded preferences, not model weights, code, keys or permissions. It does not guarantee better translation quality.

Optional checks and related reading

Advanced term trials require an explicit two-request model check and separate opt-in. Each check uses synthetic text, at most 4,096 input bytes and 1,024 requested output tokens per request, with a 15-second timeout; at most three checks per 24 hours, one minute apart. A pass lasts 30 days unless relevant settings change. It checks terms, numbers and code, not complete semantic quality.

At most three trials run, five distinct later passages per trial. Three local passes retain a term; two consecutive failures or exhaustion pause it. Each translation includes at most two terms, at most one trial, within the shared 512-byte budget. Original correction examples, duplicates and cache hits do not count. These local checks add no generation requests.

Related reading uses shared terms, code, citations or domain-supported phrases. Expand up to three links through their actual evidence. Reading links makes no model calls and does not inject history into translation prompts. Dismiss unrelated pairs or turn it off.

Jev is experimental, off by default, and needs its own GLINK generic key and endpoint. It uses jev-1-13 with /decisions, not Chat Completions. Click to review one pending suggestion, or opt into post-translation checks of up to three connections. Combined limits: 8 KiB input, three seconds, three attempts per 24 hours including failures, no retries. Identical connection checks reuse a 24-hour result. Findings never rewrite output or accept memory automatically. Existing evaluation does not establish a general translation-quality benefit; Jev is not added to ordinary translation latency.

Problems and control

HTTP 429 may mean a rate limit or exhausted quota; use the service's error details and Retry-After. Expiry of a wait enables a manual retry only. Ordinary translation times out after 90 seconds. Network delay and generation speed are separate measurements.

The UI defaults to Chinese with English and browser-language options. Themes follow the system unless specified. KnowMe is independent from Google and Saladict; third-party licenses ship with the extension.
