How do I build my first paper cache?+

Choose your topics and run an online refresh. Then use cache-only mode to revisit the same date range and filters. Cache-only mode does not contact arXiv; refresh online for the latest papers.

How do I set up page annotations?+

Author profiles and Scholar search / arXiv details have separate controls. Compact mode keeps the KnowMe button and hides long status text and author coloring. Annotations use local records and a limited bundled index; a missing match does not imply a paper is unimportant.

Does my data sync automatically?+

Saved papers, cache, personal terms and history stay in the current browser and do not sync through your Google account. Export a backup before changing devices, browser profiles or uninstalling. Your chosen model service processes translations and connection tests.
