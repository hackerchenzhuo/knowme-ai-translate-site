How do I connect a translation model?+

In quick setup, choose a provider or compatible gateway, enter the model, endpoint and key, then save and test. Requests use that service’s allowance; matching text and settings can reuse the local cache.

How do text selection and shortcuts work?+

Enable the common features in quick setup and confirm the browser permissions. Defaults: ⌘⇧Y on Mac and Alt+Shift+Y on Windows. Change the keys and global scope in the browser’s extension shortcuts page. Refresh pages that were already open after an update.

Does my data sync automatically?+

Saved papers, cache, personal terms and history stay in the current browser and do not sync through your Google account. Export a backup before changing devices, browser profiles or uninstalling. Your chosen model service processes translations and connection tests.
